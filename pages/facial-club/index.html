Why Membership

One facial is a treat.
Monthly facials change your skin.

Your skin renews itself on roughly a 28-day cycle. A single professional facial gives it a boost — but skin that's treated consistently, on a regular schedule, responds completely differently. Congestion stays clear, hydration holds, and concerns like dullness, uneven tone, and fine lines improve steadily over time instead of resetting between visits.

The Facial Club exists for exactly that reason. You get one professional facial every month, your esthetician knows your skin over time, and the cost of keeping it consistent is designed to actually be sustainable.

What You Get

Everything included in your membership

One flat monthly cost. No hidden fees, no points to track, no expiry on your facial credit.

01

One professional facial, monthly

Your monthly credit applies to any facial on the menu — chosen fresh each visit based on what your skin actually needs. Your esthetician advises, you decide.

02

Member pricing on products & add-ons

Retail Merle Norman products, treatment enhancements, and in-studio add-ons are all priced at member rates. The savings compound quickly.

03

Priority access to the schedule

Members book before the calendar opens to the public. Even on our busiest weeks, you get first pick of times — mornings, early evenings, Saturdays.

04

The same esthetician, every time

Seeing the same person monthly means they learn your skin — what changed, what's improving, what to try next. No intake form every visit. No starting over.

05

Cancel anytime, no penalties

Month-to-month, no annual contract. Give 30 days' notice to cancel. Your facial credit stays active for the current billing period regardless.

06

Three tiers. Pick your level.

How It Works

Four steps. Then it takes care of itself.

We designed the Facial Club to require as little friction as possible — one conversation to start, then your esthetician handles the rest.

01

Reach out

Reserve a beauty specialist or call (848) 229-2742. We'll answer your questions, explain the tiers, and set up your first appointment at a time that works for you.

02

Your skin assessment

Your first visit starts with a real skin assessment — not a form, an actual conversation. Your esthetician looks at your skin, asks the right questions, and selects your first treatment accordingly.

03

Membership activates

Monthly billing starts after your first visit. You're immediately given priority access to the schedule and member pricing on all in-studio products and add-ons.

04

Show up monthly

Book the same slot each month or flex it — your credit waits. Your esthetician notes what changed since last time and adjusts your treatment plan accordingly.
