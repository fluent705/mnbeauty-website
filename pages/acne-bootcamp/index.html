90-Day Acne Bootcamp — Metuchen, NJ

Clear acne, not just today's breakout

The 90-Day Acne Bootcamp is a structured three-month program that combines professional in-studio treatments with a guided home-care routine — designed to change your skin's behavior, not just surface symptoms.

Start Your Bootcamp See the 3 Phases

Most acne treatment stops at the surface

Products treat what's visible. But if the underlying cycle — congestion, excess oil production, an impaired skin barrier — isn't broken consistently over time, the acne returns. Every time.

The Bootcamp exists because a single facial can't do what ninety days of consistent, professional-level treatment can. You need enough sustained pressure on the problem to actually change how your skin behaves — not just how it looks after one appointment.

01

Products alone don't go deep enough

Over-the-counter actives work at the surface. Professional treatments penetrate deeper, clear congestion that products can't reach, and interrupt the inflammation cycle at the source.

02

Inconsistency resets every gain

Skin improves, you ease off, acne comes back. A 90-day program maintains pressure long enough for your skin to genuinely change — not just temporarily clear up.

03

No two acne cases are the same

Hormonal triggers, dietary factors, the wrong products — what's driving your breakouts is specific to you. Your esthetician assesses and adjusts both in-studio treatments and your home routine accordingly.

The Program

Three phases. Ninety days.

Each phase has a different goal. Phase 1 is different from Phase 3 — and so are the treatments and your home-care routine at each stage.

Phase 1
Days 1–30

Clear & Calm

The first month is about clearing what's there and stopping the inflammation. Your esthetician does a full skin assessment on visit one — mapping your breakout pattern, skin type, triggers, and barrier health. In-studio treatments extract existing congestion and begin calming active inflammation. Your home routine is built in this phase: the right cleanser, actives, and moisturizer chosen specifically for your skin, not a generic protocol.

Phase 1 Includes
  • Full skin assessment & intake
  • 2 acne-focused professional facials
  • Deep pore cleansing + extractions
  • Anti-inflammatory treatment protocol
  • Personalized home routine setup
  • Product recommendations for your skin
Phase 2
Days 31–60

Regulate & Rebuild

With acute inflammation settling, focus shifts to regulating oil production and rebuilding the skin barrier. This is where more advanced treatments enter — chemical exfoliants, enzyme peels, microchanneling where appropriate. You check in mid-month for a progress assessment: what's improved, what needs adjusting, and whether your home routine needs refinement. Most clients see a clear shift in both texture and breakout frequency by the end of Phase 2.

Phase 2 Includes
  • 2 professional facials (advanced protocol)
  • Enzyme or chemical peel treatment
  • Oil regulation + barrier-rebuilding focus
  • Mid-program progress check-in
  • Home routine refinement
  • Option to add Procell Microchanneling
Phase 3
Days 61–90

Maintain & Prevent

The final phase locks in results and makes sure you don't go backwards. Treatments focus on barrier strength and — if post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation or scarring is present — beginning to address that. At your final visit, you leave with a written maintenance plan: how often to come in, which products to keep using, and what to watch for. Most Bootcamp graduates join the Facial Club to hold their results long-term.

Phase 3 Includes
  • 2 professional facials (maintenance protocol)
  • Pigmentation + scar treatment where needed
  • Skin barrier strengthening focus
  • End-of-program skin assessment
  • Written long-term maintenance plan
  • Transition guidance for post-Bootcamp care
Real Results

What you'll notice — and when

Results aren't instant, but they're steady. Here's what most clients experience at each stage.

30
Days in
Phase 1 complete

Congestion clearing, inflammation calming

Active breakouts begin to reduce. Some clients notice a mild purge in weeks one and two as extractions and new actives clear deep congestion — this is normal and settles quickly. Skin starts to look less angry and more even by the end of the month.

60
Days in
Phase 2 complete

Noticeably fewer breakouts, better texture

Most clients report a meaningful shift by day 60. Breakout frequency drops, skin texture improves, and oil production tends to regulate. This is usually the phase where people start to feel genuinely confident in their skin between visits.

90
Days in
Program complete

Clearer skin, a plan to keep it that way

By day 90, the pattern that was causing your acne has been interrupted. Skin is cleaner, calmer, and the barrier is stronger. You leave with a written maintenance plan and — for most clients — a completely different relationship with your skin than when you started.

Is This Right for You?

Who the Acne Bootcamp is built for

The Bootcamp is for people who've been dealing with acne long enough to know that another product isn't the answer. It works best when you're ready to commit — not just try something.

  • Persistent or recurring acne that products haven't resolved
  • Multiple skincare routines tried without consistent improvement
  • Hormonal or adult acne — you want a professional, not another cleanser
  • Post-acne scarring or hyperpigmentation you'd like to start addressing
  • Ready to commit to 90 days of treatments and a guided home routine
If your acne is mild or occasional, a single Acne Hydrating Facial may be all you need. We'll tell you honestly at your consultation which approach makes more sense — no pressure to commit before that conversation.

Common Questions

What you'll want to know before you start

How much does the 90-Day Acne Bootcamp cost?
Bootcamp pricing is discussed during your initial consultation, as the program can vary slightly depending on your skin's specific needs and starting point. Reach out below and we'll walk you through it before you commit to anything.
Do I need to commit to all 90 days upfront?
The program is designed as a full 90-day commitment — the results depend on consistency, and dropping off early undermines the whole approach. We do a consultation first, though. There's no pressure to enrol before we've assessed whether it's right for your skin.
How often do I come in?
Twice per month for professional facials — two per phase, six total over 90 days. Each session is approximately 50–60 minutes. There's also a brief mid-program check-in between months two and three, which typically takes around 20 minutes.
Does it work for hormonal acne?
Yes — hormonal acne is one of the most common presentations we see in the Bootcamp. We can't change your hormones, but consistent professional treatment combined with the right home-care routine can significantly reduce the severity and frequency of hormonal breakouts over 90 days.
Will my skin get worse before it gets better?
Some clients experience a mild purge in the first two weeks — particularly after extractions or when introducing new active ingredients. This is normal and typically settles within 2–4 weeks. We'll set expectations based on your skin's starting point so you know what to look for.
What happens after the 90 days?
You leave your final visit with a written maintenance plan — what to keep doing, how often to come in, and what to watch for. Most Bootcamp graduates move into monthly facials to hold their results. Many join the Facial Club for the consistency and member pricing. We'll talk through the right next step at your end-of-program appointment.

Ready to give your skin 90 days of real attention?

Start with a consultation. We assess your skin, talk through your history, and tell you honestly whether the Bootcamp is the right fit.
