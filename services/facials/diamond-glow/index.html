What It Is

The glow that shows up the morning after

The Diamond Glow Microderm Facial is one of our most requested treatments — and for good reason. It does three things at once: resurfaces the skin with a precision diamond tip, clears out pores through simultaneous vacuum extraction, and replenishes what was removed with a targeted serum infusion.

Unlike traditional microdermabrasion that scatters crystals across the face, the diamond-tip handpiece gives us exact control over where we treat — so we can be aggressive where your skin needs it and gentle where it doesn't.

"Clients walk out with a visible glow — smooth texture, even tone, and skin that actually looks rested. No redness, no recovery. Just results."

Results are immediate. You leave with noticeably brighter, smoother skin — without any redness or need to hide at home. Makeup applies beautifully after, too.

The Process

Three steps. One 60-minute session.

The Diamond Glow system works all three in a single pass — no separate tools, no extra time.

01

Diamond-Tip Exfoliation

A precision diamond-encrusted tip removes dead skin cells and surface buildup layer by layer. Unlike crystal microdermabrasion, there's nothing loose on your skin — just controlled, even resurfacing that we adjust in real time based on your skin's response.

02

Vacuum Pore Extraction

Simultaneously, a gentle vacuum lifts debris, congestion, and sebum from pores as exfoliation happens. Your skin is cleared at the surface level and the follicle level at the same time — leaving pores visibly tighter and cleaner without manual extraction.

03

Targeted Serum Infusion

With your skin freshly cleared and pores open, a concentrated serum is infused directly into the dermis — going far deeper than anything you apply at home. We select the formula based on your concerns: brightening, hydrating, anti-aging, or clarifying.

What You'll See

Results you notice the same day

This isn't a treatment where you "see results in 6 weeks." Most clients notice the difference before they leave.

01

Noticeably Smoother Texture

The diamond tip removes the rough, flaky surface layer that products can't fully penetrate — leaving skin with a genuinely soft, refined feel.

02

Brighter, More Even Tone

Removing dull, dead skin and stimulating cell turnover reveals the fresher layers underneath — evening out skin tone and reducing the appearance of dark spots over time.

03

Cleaner, Tighter-Looking Pores

Vacuum extraction clears congestion without manual squeezing. Pores appear smaller and cleaner — immediately and over a series of treatments.

04

Deeper Product Absorption

With the barrier layer removed, your skincare products — and the infused serum — penetrate significantly deeper than usual, working at the level where results actually happen.

05

Visibly Reduced Fine Lines

Regular treatments stimulate collagen production, which gradually softens fine lines and improves overall skin structure over time.

06

Zero Downtime

No redness, no peeling, no hiding indoors. You can return to your day — and apply makeup — immediately after your session.

Ideal For

Your skin concerns, addressed

  • Dull, uneven skin tone that doesn't respond to your at-home routine
  • Rough or textured skin surface, including bumpy forehead or uneven cheeks
  • Clogged pores, blackheads, and mild congestion — especially on the nose and chin
  • Fine lines and early signs of aging that you'd like to slow and soften
  • Hyperpigmentation, sun spots, or post-breakout marks
  • Anyone who wants a reliable, no-downtime "refresh" before a big event
Please Wait If

When to hold off

  • Active breakouts or inflamed acne — we recommend our Acne Hydrating Facial instead
  • Rosacea flare-ups or highly reactive, sensitized skin
  • Recent use of Accutane (within the past 6 months) or aggressive retinoids
  • Open cuts, wounds, or active skin infections on the face
  • Sunburned skin — wait until fully healed before resurfacing

Not sure if this is the right treatment for you? Reach out and we'll point you in the right direction — no booking required.

Post-Treatment Care

Keep the glow going

The Diamond Glow requires almost no recovery — but a little care in the first 24 hours makes a real difference.

Do These Things

  • Apply SPF 30+ every morning — your freshly resurfaced skin is more sensitive to sun exposure
  • Keep skin hydrated with a gentle, fragrance-free moisturizer for the first 48 hours
  • Drink plenty of water to support the serum infusion and cell renewal
  • Let your skin breathe — wait 6 hours before applying makeup if possible
  • Cleanse gently with a mild, non-exfoliating cleanser for the next 3 days
  • Enjoy your glow — results continue to improve over 3–5 days as skin renews

Avoid These for 48–72 hrs

  • Direct sun exposure without SPF protection
  • Retinoids, AHAs, BHAs, or any exfoliating acids — your skin has already been resurfaced
  • Steam rooms, saunas, or intense heat treatments
  • Scratching or picking — your skin is in repair mode
  • Heavy, occlusive makeup with alcohol-based setting sprays
  • Any additional exfoliating treatments within 4 weeks

Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Does the Diamond Glow Microderm hurt?

Not at all. Most clients describe it as a gentle scratching sensation — similar to a rough brush moving across the skin. The vacuum extraction feels like a light pulling, which some people actually find satisfying. We adjust the pressure throughout the session to match your skin's sensitivity.

How long does the glow last?

The immediate brightening effect typically lasts 1–2 weeks. With consistent treatments every 4–6 weeks, the cumulative results — improved texture, clearer pores, better tone — are long-lasting and continue to improve with each session.

The serum infusion benefits, particularly hydration and brightness, tend to peak around days 3–5 as your skin settles and the active ingredients take full effect.

Can I wear makeup right after?

Yes — but we recommend waiting at least 6 hours to let your skin settle and absorb the serum fully. When you do apply makeup, you'll notice it goes on much more smoothly than usual. Many clients book this treatment the day before an event for exactly that reason.

How is this different from a regular facial?

A traditional facial uses products and massage to nourish and cleanse the skin's surface. The Diamond Glow goes one step deeper — mechanically removing the dead skin layer and delivering active ingredients below the surface barrier. The results are more immediate and more visible, and the treatment addresses texture and pore congestion in a way that a product-based facial simply can't.

How many sessions do I actually need?

You'll see results after a single session — it's genuinely one of the best treatments for a before-an-event glow. For targeted concerns like hyperpigmentation, enlarged pores, or fine lines, we recommend a series of 3 to 6 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart. After that, most clients maintain with a monthly session or as part of their Facial Club membership.
