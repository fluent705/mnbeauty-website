What It Is

Men's skin has different needs. This treatment knows that.

Men's skin is structurally different from women's — it's approximately 20% thicker, produces more sebum, and is subject to a unique set of stressors including daily shaving, ingrown hairs, and razor irritation. Most standard facials aren't designed with these differences in mind.

The Gentleman's Facial is. It uses professional-grade cleansing, steam, and targeted exfoliation to address congestion and texture issues common in men's skin, while specifically treating the irritation, ingrown hairs, and bumps caused by regular shaving. The result: cleaner, calmer, better-looking skin — without anything fussy.

"Men's skin doesn't need less care — it needs the right care. This treatment is built specifically for how male skin behaves."

No skincare knowledge required. No complicated routines to follow afterward. Just 60 minutes of expert attention and skin that looks noticeably better by the time you walk out.

The Process

Built for men's skin. Delivered in 60 minutes.

Three focused steps targeting the specific issues men's skin faces.

01

Deep Cleanse & Pore Opening

Men's skin accumulates oil and debris quickly due to higher sebum production. A professional-grade deep cleanser removes buildup that standard face wash misses, followed by steam to thoroughly open pores and soften the thicker skin structure for effective treatment.

02

Exfoliation, Extraction & Ingrown Relief

A targeted exfoliant lifts dead cells and frees trapped ingrown hairs before they become infected bumps. Professional extractions clear congestion from the beard area, nose, and forehead — zones that shaving routinely blocks. This step alone is often transformative for clients who shave regularly.

03

Hydrating Finish & Barrier Repair

Post-shave skin is often dehydrated and barrier-compromised even when it doesn't look it. A hydrating serum and lightweight moisturizer repair the barrier and restore moisture balance — leaving skin calm, comfortable, and ready for daily wear.

What You'll See

Cleaner, clearer, calmer — the results speak plainly

What consistent professional skin care does for men's skin.

01

Razor Bump Reduction

Professional exfoliation and targeted extraction free ingrown hairs before they inflame, significantly reducing the frequency and severity of razor bumps — especially around the jaw, neck, and upper lip.

02

Clearer, Less Congested Skin

Higher sebum production means men are more prone to congested pores and blackheads. Deep cleansing and professional extractions clear what daily washing leaves behind.

03

Smoother Post-Shave Surface

Exfoliation creates a smoother skin surface that makes the next week's shave noticeably closer and more comfortable, with less drag and irritation.

04

Reduced Shine and Oiliness

Deep cleansing and pore clearing reduce the excess surface oiliness that gives skin a greasy appearance by midday. Skin looks matte and clean longer.

05

Visibly Healthier Skin Tone

Regular treatment brings color evenness, reduces redness from shaving irritation, and gives skin the kind of healthy, rested appearance that's hard to describe but immediately noticeable.

06

Zero Maintenance Required

You don't need a new skincare routine to maintain results. Monthly sessions keep the skin clear, and your existing routine works better because you're not fighting congestion and buildup.

Ideal For

Your skin concerns, addressed

  • Men with oily, combination, or congested skin that feels dull or looks tired
  • Anyone who shaves regularly and struggles with razor bumps, ingrown hairs, or irritation
  • Skin with clogged pores, blackheads, or persistent congestion in the beard area
  • First-time facial clients who want a no-fuss, results-focused experience
  • Men preparing for an event — a wedding, shoot, or important occasion
  • Anyone whose skincare routine has plateaued and isn't making visible progress
Please Wait If

When to hold off

  • Active, severely inflamed or infected ingrown hairs — allow healing before treatment
  • Significant razor burn covering large areas of the face — wait for skin to recover
  • Active skin infections, open wounds, or cold sores on the face
  • Severe rosacea flares — gentle approaches are better during active flares
  • Recent chemical peels or resurfacing treatments within 2 weeks

Not sure if this is the right treatment? Reach out and we’ll point you in the right direction — no booking required.

Post-Treatment Care

Take care of the work

Give the treatment 24 hours to fully settle and you'll see the best results.

Do These Things

  • Skip shaving for at least 24 hours to allow pores and any extracted areas to close fully
  • Apply SPF before going outdoors — even if you rarely use it normally
  • Use a gentle, fragrance-free moisturizer for the first 48 hours
  • When you do shave next, use a fresh blade and a quality shave gel — you'll notice the difference
  • Book monthly to maintain clear pores and keep ingrown hairs from re-establishing

Avoid These for 48–72 hrs

  • Shaving for 24 hours post-treatment — pores need time to close
  • Heavy gym sessions or excessive sweating on the day of treatment
  • Alcohol-based aftershaves or astringents for 48 hours
  • Picking or touching extracted areas — let them heal cleanly
  • Heavy, comedogenic products on freshly cleared skin

Common Questions

Everything you need to know

I've never had a facial. What should I expect?

You'll lie comfortably while your specialist works on your skin for 60 minutes. There's steam, cleansing products, a bit of pressure during extractions, and a mask. Some parts are relaxing, some feel like productive pressure. You leave with noticeably clearer, cleaner skin. Most first-timers wonder why they waited.

How soon before an event should I book?

5–7 days before is ideal — your skin has fully settled and looks its best. The day before works too for a simple refresh, but if extractions are needed, allow a day or two for any minor redness to resolve.

Will the treatment irritate skin that's already sensitive from shaving?

No — the protocol is calibrated for post-shave skin. We use non-stripping, barrier-safe products throughout. If you have significant active irritation on the day, we adjust accordingly. The goal is to calm and repair, not add stress.

How often should I come in?

Monthly is the sweet spot for most clients. It clears new congestion before it becomes problematic and manages ingrown hair buildup before it gets uncomfortable. Clients who commit to monthly sessions typically see a significant improvement in skin quality within 3 visits.

Do I need to change my skincare routine after?

Not necessarily. Your specialist can recommend simple additions if something specific would help — but there's no obligation. Many clients simply use a basic cleanser and moisturizer and rely on monthly sessions to handle the deeper work.
