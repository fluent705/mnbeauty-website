What It Is

Three technologies. One session. Results that compound.

The Smart Lift is our most sophisticated facial — a precisely sequenced combination of microcurrent muscle toning, radiofrequency collagen stimulation, and LED light therapy. Each technology addresses a different layer of the aging process, and together they produce a cumulative effect that no single treatment can match.

This is the treatment for clients who want clinical-grade results from a spa setting — the lifting and firming that previously required invasive procedures, delivered through cutting-edge technology with zero downtime, no recovery, and results that continue to improve between sessions.

"We combine three modalities because aging is not a single-layer problem. Microcurrent works the muscle; RF works the collagen; LED works the cellular level. All three, every session."

The Smart Lift is the cornerstone of our most results-driven clients' routines — often paired with Diamond Glow or Dermaplaning on alternating months for a comprehensive approach to skin health and aging prevention.

The Process

Three technologies, three layers, one outstanding result.

Sequenced specifically so each phase prepares the skin for the next.

01

Microcurrent Muscle Toning

Low-level electrical currents — completely painless, imperceptible to most clients — stimulate the facial muscles in precise patterns that re-educate them toward a lifted, more defined position. Think of it as a workout for your facial muscles. ATP production is increased, which accelerates cellular repair and collagen activity. The immediate effect is a subtle but genuine lift — most visible along the brow, cheekbones, and jawline.

02

Multi-Polar RF Collagen Stimulation

With microcirculation elevated from the microcurrent phase, RF energy penetrates the dermis at its most receptive. The controlled thermal energy tightens existing collagen fibers immediately and triggers a long-term fibroblast response that produces new collagen over 6 to 12 weeks. Combined with microcurrent, the firming effect is significantly more pronounced than either technology achieves alone.

03

LED Light Therapy Finish

Red LED light (630–660nm) boosts cellular energy production, accelerates the collagen synthesis initiated by RF, and reduces inflammation. Blue LED targets any residual surface bacteria. The LED phase extends and deepens the benefits of the two preceding steps — sending you home with skin that's not just treated, but actively healing and regenerating.

What You'll See

The results of three treatments in one

What clinical-grade multi-technology skin care actually delivers.

01

Visible Lift & Redefined Contour

Microcurrent re-educates facial muscles toward a lifted position, improving the brow arch, cheekbone prominence, and jawline definition in ways that neither product nor single-modality treatments can replicate.

02

Deep Collagen Rebuilding

RF energy combined with microcurrent-elevated ATP creates an optimal environment for collagen synthesis. New collagen growth shows progressively — at weeks 4, 8, and 12 — continuing to improve long after each session.

03

Firmer, Tighter Skin

The immediate thermal contraction of RF energy, amplified by the preceding microcurrent stimulation, produces a firming effect that is visible and measurable. Skin feels structurally different — denser and more elastic.

04

Accelerated Cellular Renewal

Red LED light triggers mitochondrial activity and increases ATP production — essentially energizing every skin cell to function at a higher level. Cell turnover accelerates, repair processes speed up, and the skin's overall health improves.

05

Reduced Wrinkles & Expression Lines

The combination of muscle re-education, collagen rebuilding, and enhanced cellular renewal addresses wrinkles from multiple angles — softening existing lines while slowing the formation of new ones.

06

Zero Downtime, Cumulative Results

No redness, no recovery, no hiding. You can go directly to dinner, an event, or a photoshoot. And unlike single-session treatments, every Smart Lift compounds on the last — the results at session 6 are significantly better than at session 1.

Please Wait If

When to hold off

  • Pacemakers or implanted electrical devices — both microcurrent and RF are contraindicated
  • Epilepsy — microcurrent electrical stimulation is not recommended
  • Metal implants (plates, screws) in the facial treatment area
  • Pregnancy — consult your OB before any multi-energy device treatment
  • Fillers or Botox injected within the past 2 weeks — allow full settling before RF or microcurrent

Not sure if this is the right treatment? Reach out and we’ll point you in the right direction — no booking required.

Post-Treatment Care

Protect the lift

The collagen-building has begun — support it over the next 72 hours.

Do These Things

  • Apply SPF 30+ every morning in the days following treatment — collagen-active skin is more UV-sensitive
  • Use a peptide and growth-factor serum for the 3 nights following treatment — cellular uptake is elevated
  • Stay well hydrated — collagen synthesis is supported by good systemic hydration
  • Sleep on your back for the first 2 nights to maintain the lifted contour
  • Book your next session at 4 to 6 weeks to stay within the optimal collagen-building cycle

Avoid These for 48 to 72 hrs

  • Heat treatments — saunas, steam rooms, or hot yoga — for 24 hours
  • Exfoliating acids or retinoids for 48 hours post-session
  • Intense exercise causing significant flushing on the day of treatment
  • Any additional energy-based device treatments within 4 weeks
  • Sun exposure without SPF — RF-stimulated collagen is temporarily more photosensitive

Common Questions

Everything you need to know

How does this compare to injectables like Botox or fillers?

Botox relaxes specific muscles; fillers add volume. The Smart Lift rebuilds collagen structure, re-educates muscle positioning, and improves cellular health — working with your skin's own biology rather than modifying or supplementing it. Results are more gradual and natural-looking. Many clients use the Smart Lift proactively to delay or reduce the frequency of injectable treatments.

Will I feel anything during the treatment?

Microcurrent: a very mild tingle or nothing at all — most clients don't feel it. RF: a pleasant, controlled warmth, similar to a hot stone massage in targeted zones. LED: completely imperceptible — just light. The overall experience is closer to a relaxing spa treatment than a clinical procedure.

How many sessions do I actually need?

You'll notice the immediate lift after your first session. For meaningful, lasting structural improvement — visible firming, collagen rebuilding, and redefined contour — a series of 6 is recommended. After completing a series, most clients maintain with monthly sessions or as part of their Facial Club membership.

Can I combine this with other treatments?

Yes — many clients alternate the Smart Lift with Diamond Glow or Dermaplaning on a monthly basis: resurfacing treatments on even months, Smart Lift on odd months. This creates a comprehensive protocol addressing both skin quality and structural aging simultaneously. We're happy to design a treatment plan during your first session.

When will I see the full results?

There's an immediate lift visible after each session. The collagen rebuild shows progressively — you'll notice significant improvement at the 6-week mark between sessions, with cumulative enhancement building over a series. Most clients see their best results 6 to 8 weeks after completing a series of 6.
