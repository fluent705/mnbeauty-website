What It Is

The smoothest your skin has ever felt.

Dermaplaning uses a sterile, surgical-grade blade — wielded by a trained esthetician in precise, feather-light strokes — to remove the entire layer of dead skin cells and vellus hair (peach fuzz) from the face. The result is an immediate, dramatic smoothness that no skincare product can replicate.

The difference from other exfoliation methods: dermaplaning is purely physical, works on all skin types including sensitive skin, and produces zero chemical reaction — making it one of the most universally safe resurfacing treatments available. There's no downtime, no inflammation risk from active ingredients, and nothing to wait out.

"When the blade passes, so does everything that was dulling your skin — dead cells, fine hairs, product residue. What's left is a genuinely different skin surface."

Makeup application immediately after dermaplaning is notably different — foundation sits flush, concealer blends effortlessly, and the overall look is more polished and skin-like. This is why it's a top choice for brides and clients preparing for photoshoots.

The Process

Prep, resurface, protect — in 60 precise minutes.

Three steps for a level of smoothness your skincare routine cannot achieve alone.

01

Oil-Free Prep & Dry Cleanse

Dermaplaning requires completely dry, oil-free skin for the blade to work safely and effectively. Your specialist performs a thorough two-phase cleanse, removes all product residue and natural oils, and prepares the skin surface so every stroke of the blade has clean, consistent contact.

02

Precision Surgical Dermaplaning

Using a sterile No.10 surgical blade held at a precise 45-degree angle, your esthetician works across the face in careful, upward strokes — removing the stratum corneum (dead skin layer) and all vellus hair simultaneously. The technique is methodical and adapted in real time to the contours of your face, avoiding active blemishes and sensitive zones.

03

Active Serum Infusion & Protect

Immediately after dermaplaning, the skin's absorption capacity increases by over 70% — meaning whatever goes on next penetrates significantly deeper than usual. A targeted active serum (hydrating, brightening, or antiaging depending on your skin) is applied and massaged in, followed by a protective moisturizer and SPF.

What You'll See

Glass skin — immediately, visibly, undeniably

What happens when every barrier between your skin and the world is removed.

01

Instantly Glass-Smooth Skin

Nothing creates smoothness like removing the rough, textured surface layer entirely. Clients run their fingers across their face after treatment and are genuinely surprised — it's a different texture than anything they've experienced from their home routine.

02

Complete Peach Fuzz Removal

Vellus hair — the fine, light fuzz that covers most faces — traps foundation, catches light unfavorably, and creates texture. Dermaplaning removes it entirely. Skin looks cleaner, foundation sits differently, and photos look different.

03

Dramatically Better Product Absorption

With the dead cell layer removed, skincare products penetrate over 70% more effectively. The serums you apply at home in the week after treatment actually work — not just sit on the surface.

04

Brighter, More Even Tone

Removing dead skin cells reveals the fresher, more evenly pigmented layers beneath. Skin looks brighter and more even without any active brightening ingredients.

05

Flawless Makeup Base

Makeup artists and photographers know: the best canvas is freshly dermaplaned skin. Foundation looks like skin, not makeup. Concealer sits without creasing. The result photographs beautifully.

06

Zero Chemical Reaction

Unlike acid peels or enzyme treatments, dermaplaning involves no active ingredients — which means no risk of irritation, no inflammatory response, and no restrictions for sensitive skin. It's the purest form of physical exfoliation.

Please Wait If

When to hold off

  • Active acne breakouts — blade contact with inflamed lesions spreads bacteria; wait until clear
  • Rosacea during a flare — dermaplaning can aggravate active rosacea; book during remission
  • Extremely thin or compromised skin — consult your specialist before booking
  • Keratosis pilaris on the face — requires a different treatment approach
  • Psoriasis or eczema in the treatment area — blade contact can worsen flares

Not sure if this is the right treatment? Reach out and we’ll point you in the right direction — no booking required.

Post-Treatment Care

Protect what you just revealed

Fresh dermaplaned skin absorbs everything — protect it from the sun, nourish it with actives.

Do These Things

  • Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30+ every morning — freshly dermaplaned skin has no dead cell UV protection
  • Use a gentle, fragrance-free moisturizer for the first 48 hours to keep skin comfortable
  • Apply your best serum immediately after treatment and for the next 3 nights — absorption is at peak
  • Enjoy the smoothness — and notice how differently makeup applies for the next 3–4 weeks
  • Book your next session at 4–6 weeks to maintain the skin surface before hair and dead cells return

Avoid These for 48–72 hrs

  • Direct sun exposure without SPF — unprotected dermaplaned skin burns faster
  • Exfoliating acids, retinoids, or physical scrubs for 5 days — the surface is already cleared
  • Heavy, occlusive makeup for the first 8 hours — pores are open and receptive
  • Hot showers, steam rooms, or saunas for 24 hours
  • Touching the face excessively — the smooth surface is genuinely tempting but hands carry bacteria

Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Will my hair grow back thicker after dermaplaning?

No — this is the most common concern and the most thoroughly debunked myth. Vellus hair (peach fuzz) has no follicle capable of producing thicker, darker hair. Dermaplaning removes vellus hair at the surface — it grows back the same as before. The regrowth may feel slightly blunt at first but quickly returns to its natural soft texture.

Does dermaplaning hurt?

Not at all. Most clients describe it as an unusual sensation — a light scraping or scratching — that becomes almost meditative once they relax into it. There is no pain. Some clients fall asleep during the treatment.

Can I wear makeup afterward?

Yes — but wait at least 8 hours. After that, makeup applies in a way that feels noticeably different: smoother, more blended, more skin-like. Many clients describe the first makeup application post-dermaplaning as the best their skin has ever looked with makeup on.

How long do results last?

The smoothness is most dramatic immediately and maintains well for 3–4 weeks. Vellus hair begins to return at around 4 weeks, and the dead skin layer rebuilds over the same period. Most clients book every 4–6 weeks to stay ahead of regrowth.

Can I dermaplane if I have acne?

It depends. Comedonal acne (blackheads, whiteheads, non-inflamed) is generally fine to treat around. Active inflammatory acne (red, raised, painful lesions) is a contraindication — blade contact spreads bacteria. If you're unsure, describe your skin to us when booking and we'll advise.
