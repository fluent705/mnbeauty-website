What It Is

Small dots, significant tightening

Fibroblast treatment uses a plasma pen, a device that arcs a small, controlled point of plasma just above the skin’s surface, creating a tiny thermal contraction point without cutting or injecting anything. Each point causes an immediate, visible tightening, and the skin’s healing response builds further collagen in the weeks after. It’s one of the most targeted treatments we offer, typically used on smaller, specific zones rather than the whole face.

"You watch the skin tighten in real time, it’s one of the few treatments where the effect isn’t subtle."

This is the treatment we reach for on upper eyelids, under-eye crepiness, lip lines, and other small areas where surgery feels like overkill but skincare alone isn’t going to cut it.

The Process

Point by Point

Precision is the entire treatment.

01

Numb

A topical anesthetic is applied and given time to fully take effect, since this treatment works directly on the skin’s surface.

02

Treat

The plasma pen is used point by point across the treatment zone, each contact creating a tiny, controlled contraction.

03

Protect

A soothing balm is applied, and we walk you through exactly how to care for the small carbon crusts that will form over the next few days.

Ideal For

For the areas that need precision, not volume

  • You have a specific small area, eyelids, under-eyes, lip lines, bothering you more than your whole face
  • You want a longer-lasting result than injectables typically provide in that area
  • You’re comfortable with visible small crusting for about a week during healing
  • You have lighter to medium skin tones (we’ll assess your tone honestly at consultation)
  • You don’t currently have keloid scarring tendencies
  • You can avoid sun exposure on the treated area during healing
Please Wait If

When to hold off

  • You’re pregnant or nursing
  • You have a history of keloid or hypertrophic scarring
  • You have active skin infections, cold sores, or open wounds in the treatment area
  • You have diabetes or a condition that significantly impairs healing, we’ll want to discuss this first
  • You have deeper skin tones prone to post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, we’ll do an honest assessment at consultation before recommending this treatment

Not sure if this is the right treatment? Reach out and we’ll point you in the right direction, no booking required.

Post-Treatment Care

The most involved aftercare on this page, and the most important

Small carbon crusts form at each treatment point and need to shed naturally over about a week. How you care for them determines your final result.

Do These Things

  • Keep the treated area completely clean and dry, especially the first 48 hours
  • Apply the healing ointment we provide exactly as directed
  • Let every crust fall off entirely on its own, this can take up to 10 days
  • Sleep with your head slightly elevated for the first couple of nights if the treated area is near your eyes
  • Wear a wide-brim hat or seek shade if you’re outdoors during healing

Avoid These for 7 to 10 days

  • Picking, scratching, or pulling at any crust, this is the single biggest risk to your result
  • Makeup or skincare products on the treated area until crusts have fully shed
  • Direct sun exposure on the treated area for several weeks after
  • Sweating, swimming, or steam directly on the treated zone during healing
  • Touching the area with unwashed hands

Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Is this the same as the Jet Cold Plasma treatment?

No, Jet Cold Plasma is a non-contact stream that never touches your skin, with no downtime. Fibroblast uses a pen that briefly contacts the skin at each point, creating small crusts that shed over about a week. They’re both “plasma” but work very differently.

Will there be visible marks after treatment?

Yes, small, dark dot-like crusts form at each treatment point and are visible for roughly 5 to 10 days while they heal and shed. Most clients plan this around time off or a period they can avoid close-up social situations.

Is this safe for my skin tone?

We assess this honestly at your consultation. Fibroblast carries a higher risk of post-treatment pigment changes on deeper skin tones, and we’d rather recommend a different treatment than risk a result you’re not happy with.

How long do results last?

Typically 1 to 3 years per treated zone, making this one of the longer-lasting non-surgical options we offer.

Does it hurt?

We numb the area thoroughly beforehand. Most clients feel warmth and light snapping sensations rather than sharp pain, though sensitivity varies by area, eyelids tend to be more sensitive than the jawline.
