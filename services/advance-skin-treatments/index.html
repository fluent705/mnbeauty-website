Bio Re Peel Face Treatment

BioRePeel works via biostimulation technology — encouraging collagen production, refining texture, and addressing pigmentation and fine lines without harsh surface peeling. A sophisticated chemical formulation that works at multiple skin depths. Suitable for most skin types with minimal downtime.

Acne Lift Peel

A targeted chemical peel formulated for acne-prone skin — clearing congestion, reducing active breakouts, and refining the surface texture left behind by past blemishes. Salicylic and other keratolytic acids work into the pore to address the problem at its source, not just the surface.

Lactic Acid Brightening Peel

Lactic acid exfoliates at the surface while drawing moisture into the skin — making it both effective and gentler than stronger AHAs. Noticeably brighter, more even skin afterward. A good starting point for anyone new to chemical peels, and well-suited for dry or sensitive skin types.

Triple Bright Peel

The Glytone Triple Bright Peel is a medium-depth in-office chemical peel formulated with 20% TCA — working at three levels to address hyperpigmentation, sun damage, and uneven tone. A meaningful step up from lighter peels for those ready for more visible, lasting brightening results.
