What It Is

Ultrasound, focused where it counts

HIFU delivers focused ultrasound energy past the surface of the skin, straight to the same deep structural layer surgeons address in a facelift, without cutting anything. That energy triggers a genuine tightening response as your body heals the treated layer over the following months, which is why HIFU is often called the closest non-surgical treatment to a facelift.

"We’re not tightening the surface. We’re going after the layer that actually holds your face up."

Because it works at that depth, most clients need far fewer sessions than surface treatments, often just one full treatment provides results that continue improving for months.

Ideal For

For moderate laxity, not surgical-level sagging

  • You have moderate skin laxity through the cheeks, jaw, or neck
  • You want facelift-adjacent results without surgery or injectables
  • You’re comfortable waiting a few months to see the full result
  • You want a treatment you don’t have to repeat often
  • You’re in good general health with no contraindicating conditions
  • You have realistic expectations, this firms and lifts, it doesn’t replace surgery for significant sagging
Please Wait If

When to hold off

  • You’re pregnant or nursing
  • You have a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device
  • You have severe skin laxity that would likely need a surgical consult instead, we’ll be honest with you about this
  • You have active cystic acne, open wounds, or infection in the treatment area
  • You have metal implants or fillers in the direct treatment path, tell us so we can plan around them

Not sure if this is the right treatment? Reach out and we’ll point you in the right direction, no booking required.

Post-Treatment Care

Little to do, but know what’s normal

Downtime is minimal, but understanding the timeline helps you judge your results fairly.

Do These Things

  • Apply a gentle moisturizer if skin feels tender or tight
  • Take an over-the-counter pain reliever if you’re sore, as you would after a deep massage
  • Be patient, real results build over 8 to 12 weeks, not overnight
  • Wear SPF daily, as always
  • Reach out if swelling or tenderness seems more than expected

Avoid These for 24 hours

  • Very hot showers, saunas, or steam rooms for 24 hours
  • Strenuous exercise the day of treatment
  • Comparing your results to next-week photos, this treatment needs months, not days
  • Additional energy-based treatments in the same area until we’ve reassessed
  • Skipping your scheduled follow-up check, which helps us track your results properly
Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Is this actually as effective as a facelift?

No, and we won’t tell you otherwise, surgery moves and removes tissue in ways ultrasound can’t. What HIFU does very well is tighten and lift skin with moderate laxity, non-surgically. For significant sagging, we’ll say so honestly at your consultation.

When will I see results?

Some initial tightening is visible within a few weeks, but the full result builds over 2 to 3 months as your body remodels the treated tissue.

Does it hurt?

There’s a distinct warm, prickling sensation as the ultrasound energy is delivered at each point, most clients tolerate it well, but it’s more sensation than something like RF treatments. We can adjust intensity if it’s too much.

How long does one treatment last?

Most clients hold their results for 12 to 18 months before considering a touch-up, since natural aging continues in the meantime.

Can I combine this with other treatments?

We typically recommend spacing HIFU apart from other energy-based treatments in the same area. It pairs well with skincare-focused treatments like Chemical Peels on a separate visit.
