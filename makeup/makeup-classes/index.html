Makeup Classes in Metuchen, NJ — Private & Group

Learn makeup directly from our team — one-on-one or in a small group. From everyday basics to special occasion looks, classes are hands-on and personalized to your skill level.

Class Options & Pricing

All classes are hands-on at the studio and taught directly on your face with your own products. Because it's personalized, what you learn is actually applicable — not generic technique that doesn't suit your features or skin type.

Makeup Class — Full Face
A comprehensive one-on-one class covering the full face — foundation, concealer, contouring, eye techniques, brows, and lip. You leave knowing how to recreate your entire personal makeup look. Best for anyone wanting to build proper technique from the ground up.

Makeup Class — Single Focus
