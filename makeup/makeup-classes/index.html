Common Questions

What to know before you book

Do I need experience before my first class?

Not at all. Our First-Timer Lesson starts from zero — no experience required, no judgment. Many students learn more in 60 minutes than they did from years of self-teaching. We meet you exactly where you are.

Should I bring my own makeup products?

We recommend it for private lessons — learning with your own products means you can replicate the lesson at home exactly. We also have a full professional product range you can try. Bring everything and we’ll go through it together.

How is this different from watching tutorials online?

Tutorials show you one person’s technique on one person’s face. Your instructor looks at your face, watches your application in real time, and corrects the specific things that don’t work for your features. “Your blush is sitting too low for your face shape” is something no video can tell you. The difference in learning speed is significant.

What age groups do you teach?

All ages are welcome. Our youngest students are typically teens (13+) getting their first lesson. Our oldest are in their 70s, updating technique and adapting to how makeup interacts with aging skin. We adjust the approach based on the client, not a fixed curriculum.

Can I book a class as a gift?

Yes — makeup classes are one of our most popular gifts. Contact us to arrange a gift class and we’ll provide a gift note the recipient can schedule at their convenience. Perfect for birthdays, Mother’s Day, bridal party gifts, and graduations.

What if I want to focus on one specific technique?

Tell us when you book. Private lessons are completely customizable — if you want to learn sharp liner, how to make eyes look more open, or how to get foundation to last all day, we build the session around exactly that. No standard curriculum required.
