Common Questions

Everything you need to know

How far in advance should I book?

For bridal services, 6 to 12 months in advance for peak spring and fall dates. For special events, 2 to 4 weeks is usually enough. Last-minute bookings are taken when availability allows — always worth calling.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Come with a clean, moisturized face and no makeup if possible. Bring any inspiration photos — screenshots are fine. If you have specific products we need to use, bring those too. Everything else we provide.

How long does makeup last for a full-day event?

We use professional long-wear and waterproof formulas. With proper setting, bridal makeup is designed to last 10 to 14 hours through tears, humidity, dancing, and outdoor conditions. You leave with a touch-up kit for lipstick and blotting.

Do you offer on-location services?

We primarily work from our Metuchen studio. For bridal parties and larger groups, on-location may be available — contact us to discuss and we’ll do our best to accommodate your situation.

What products do you use?

Primarily Merle Norman’s professional makeup line — trusted for coverage, longevity, and color range. We’ll provide a full product list after your appointment so you can maintain the look at home.

Is a bridal trial really necessary?

Strongly recommended. The trial session lets you see the look in advance, make adjustments without time pressure, and arrive on your wedding morning knowing exactly what to expect. Most brides who skip it wish they hadn’t.
